Assumptions & sources

  • Default rate 6.7% — Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 6.66% the week of July 30, 2026.
  • Property tax 1.1%/yr and insurance 0.55%/yr of home value are national-average defaults — set your county's real numbers. Both can also be entered as a flat $/yr amount if you know your actual bill.
  • Qualifying ratios: conventional 28/36 (lender max 36/45), FHA 31/43 (lender max 40/50), VA back-end 41 (max 50). FHA adds 1.75% upfront MIP (financed) plus 0.55%/yr MIP; VA adds a 2.15% financed funding fee (first use) and no monthly MI.
  • Minimum down payment enforced: 3% conventional, 3.5% FHA, 0% VA.
  • 2026 conforming loan limit: $832,750 baseline ($1,249,125 ceiling in high-cost counties). FHA county limits vary — floor ≈ $541,300.
