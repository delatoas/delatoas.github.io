Assumptions & sources

  • Default rate 6.7% — Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 6.66% the week of July 30, 2026. The same rate is used for all three loan types for comparability, though FHA/VA often clear a bit lower in practice since they're government-backed.
  • Property tax 1.1%/yr and insurance 0.55%/yr of home value are national-average defaults — set your county's real numbers. Both can also be entered as a flat $/yr amount if you know your actual bill. The amortization schedule holds P&I only; it doesn't project tax, insurance, or HOA growth over the loan's life.
  • Qualifying ratios: conventional 28/36 (lender max 36/45), FHA 31/43 (lender max 40/50), VA back-end 41 (max 50). FHA adds 1.75% upfront MIP (financed) plus 0.55%/yr MIP, cancelling after 11 years with 10%+ down or lasting the life of the loan otherwise. VA adds a financed funding fee tiered by down payment (1.25% at 10%+, 1.5% at 5–9.99%, 2.15%/3.3% under 5% for first use/reuse) and waived with a VA disability rating; no monthly MI.
  • Minimum down payment enforced: 3% conventional, 3.5% FHA, 0% VA — rising to 10% for conventional loans priced as jumbo.
  • 2026 conforming loan limit: $832,750 baseline ($1,249,125 ceiling in high-cost counties). Above it, conventional loans are priced as jumbo: rate + 0.25 points and 10%+ down — a representative market spread, not a specific lender's rate sheet. FHA county limits vary — floor ≈ $541,300; VA has no limit with full entitlement, so it's never treated as jumbo here.
